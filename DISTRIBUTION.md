# DISTRIBUTION — staged plan (NOT executed this pass)

This pass proves the factory can *take money* (TEST mode). It does **not** run distribution and does
**not** chase a real customer. This document stages how DOT Navigator would earn awareness once the
founder flips go-live (see `GO-LIVE.md`). Nothing here runs autonomously without the founder.

## ICP
First-18-month new-entrant carriers and owner-operators — someone who just received a USDOT number and is
staring down the mandatory new-entrant **safety audit** with no idea of the sequence. High intent, real
deadline, underserved (incumbents store documents or sell managed services they can't afford).

## The hook = the free artifact
The **free dated roadmap** (the navigator) is the lead magnet. Every channel leads with *their* verdict /
*their* roadmap — value first, before any ask. The paid unlock is the natural next step, not the pitch.

## The four channels (per the factory distribution doctrine)
1. **Inbound** — the free roadmap is shareable on its own; SEO around "new entrant safety audit checklist",
   "FMCSA 18 month compliance", "new authority what's due when".
2. **Warm / contextual** — answer the exact question where carriers already ask it (r/Truckers,
   r/owneroperators, TruckersReport, new-authority Facebook groups), linking the free roadmap when it
   genuinely answers the thread — not drive-by spam.
3. **Cold awareness** — reach new-authority holders (public FMCSA registrations) with a genuinely useful
   free artifact (their dated roadmap), strategic and small-scale, never bulk.
4. **Organic amplifier** — the printable packet, the countdown, and "here's your first 18 months in one
   page" are inherently shareable in carrier communities.

## The governance line (the andon cord for distribution)
Every touch must clear ALL of:
- **Honest identity** — no fake demos, no impersonation, clearly The Native Factory.
- **Value-first** — lead with something useful (their roadmap) on every contact.
- **Instant opt-out** — honored immediately, no friction.
- **Hands-off their systems** — never access or modify a prospect's platform/account.
- **Legal** — CAN-SPAM / GDPR / platform & forum rules respected; targeted, not junk.

This refuses only deception, intrusion, and ignored opt-outs — not outreach itself.
